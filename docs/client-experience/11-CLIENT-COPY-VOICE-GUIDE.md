# Client Experience: Copy, Voice & Tone Guide

## Document Overview
This document outlines the complete copy, voice, and tone guidelines for all client-facing text within the CareConnect platform. It provides the foundation for the "Care Concierge" experience, ensuring every touchpoint feels reassuring, professional, and clear.

## Business Context & Positioning

- **Platform Name:** CareConnect
- **Domain:** Healthcare Staffing & Home Care
- **Location Context:** Delhi NCR (Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad)
- **Primary Tone:** CARE CONCIERGE — warm, reassuring, precise, trustworthy, professional but not clinical-cold.
- **Currency Format:** INR (₹)
- **Language Standards:** English (Indian English conventions)
- **Strictly AVOID:** Marketplace language (freelancers, gigs, orders), tech jargon (algorithms, matches), clinical codes, insurance speak, and cold bureaucratic phrasing.

---

## 1. Voice Principles

The CareConnect voice must consistently reflect our brand values. We are managing people's health and the care of their loved ones, which is a high-stakes, emotionally sensitive environment.

### Warm but not casual
- **Do:** "We're here to help you find the right care for your loved one."
- **Don't:** "Hey there! Let's get you hooked up with a provider!"
- **Why:** Health concerns require respect. Empathy is good; inappropriate familiarity is not.

### Precise but not clinical
- **Do:** "Your caregiver will arrive between 9:00 AM and 10:00 AM."
- **Don't:** "ETA 0900hrs. Subject to vital sign triage."
- **Why:** Clients need clarity without being overwhelmed by medical jargon.

### Reassuring but not patronizing
- **Do:** "Your care request has been received. Our operations team is reviewing it to ensure the best assignment."
- **Don't:** "Don't worry your pretty little head, we've got it covered!"
- **Why:** Clients want to feel secure in their choice without feeling dismissed.

### Professional but not corporate
- **Do:** "Please review the care plan before confirming your booking."
- **Don't:** "Pursuant to our terms of service, execute the confirmation of the deliverables."
- **Why:** We are a trusted partner, not a faceless institution.

### Clear but not dumbed-down
- **Do:** "Physiotherapy for post-operative knee replacement recovery."
- **Don't:** "Leg fixing exercises."
- **Why:** Our clients are intelligent adults seeking professional medical services.

## 4. Date & Number Formatting

Consistent formatting is crucial for trust and clarity.

- **Dates:** Always use the format `Day, DD Mon YYYY` to avoid ambiguity.
  - *Example:* "Mon, 20 Mar 2026"
  - *Incorrect:* "20/03/2026", "March 20th"
- **Times:** Always use 12-hour format with AM/PM capitalized. Use a hyphen for ranges with spaces on either side.
  - *Example:* "10:00 AM - 08:00 PM"
  - *Incorrect:* "10-8pm", "10:00-20:00"
- **Currency:** Always prefix with the Indian Rupee symbol (₹). Use commas for the Indian numbering system (lakhs/crores).
  - *Example:* "₹1,200 / visit", "₹12,500"
  - *Incorrect:* "Rs. 1200", "12,500 INR"
- **Phone Numbers:** Use the country code and hyphenate for readability.
  - *Example:* "+91-98765-43210"
  - *Incorrect:* "919876543210", "09876543210"
- **Booking Codes:** Standardized prefix, year, and alphanumeric code.
  - *Example:* "BKG-2026-X7A9"
  - *Incorrect:* "booking 123", "#X7A9"

## 5. Words to USE vs Words to AVOID

To maintain our premium, managed-care positioning, strictly adhere to this vocabulary.

| USE | AVOID | Reason / Context |
|-----|-------|------------------|
| Care request | Order | We deal with healthcare, not e-commerce commodities. |
| Service | Product | We provide human-delivered care. |
| Staff member / Care Professional | Freelancer / Contractor | We manage our workforce; they represent CareConnect. |
| Care team | Provider | "Provider" sounds clinical and bureaucratic. |
| Assigned | Matched | "Matched" sounds like a dating app or algorithm. |
| Operations team | Algorithm | Emphasize human oversight and care management. |
| Home care | Gig / Task | Respect the gravity of medical and attendant work. |
| Patient | User / Consumer | Use "client" for the person paying, "patient" for the person receiving care. |
| Booking | Transaction | "Transaction" diminishes the relationship. |
| Confirmation | Acceptance | "Acceptance" implies the staff could have rejected it like a ride-share. |
| Care Assessment | Triage | "Triage" implies an emergency room environment. |
| Care Plan | Scope of Work | "Scope of work" is for construction, not nursing. |
| Consultation | Interview | We consult to assess needs, we don't interview clients. |

## 6. General Grammar & Mechanics

- **Capitalization:** Use sentence case for most UI elements (labels, buttons, tooltips). Title case is reserved for Page Titles, Navigation Links, and major Section Headers.
  - *Sentence case:* "Book a service", "Patient details"
  - *Title case:* "My Bookings", "Client Dashboard"
- **Punctuation in UI:** Avoid terminal punctuation (periods) in buttons, short list items, and labels. Use periods in full sentences within descriptions, tooltips, and empty state messages.
- **Active Voice:** Write in the active voice to ensure clarity and directness.
  - *Active:* "Our operations team will review your request."
  - *Passive (Avoid):* "Your request will be reviewed by our operations team."
- **Pronouns:** Speak directly to the client using "you" and "your". Refer to CareConnect as "we", "us", or "our team".

## 7. Placeholder Text Guidelines

Placeholder text inside input fields should provide a clear example of the expected format, not just repeat the label.

- **Name Input:** *Label:* Full Name | *Placeholder:* e.g., Ramesh Kumar
- **Phone Input:** *Label:* Mobile Number | *Placeholder:* e.g., +91-98765-43210
- **Email Input:** *Label:* Email Address | *Placeholder:* e.g., name@example.com
- **Address Input:** *Label:* Street Address | *Placeholder:* House/Flat No., Building Name
- **Search Bar:** *Placeholder:* Search for nursing, physiotherapy...

## 8. Error Message Guidelines

Errors happen, but they should never cause panic or assign blame to the client.

- **Rule 1: Be specific.** Explain exactly what went wrong. (Don't say "Invalid input")
- **Rule 2: Provide a solution.** Tell them how to fix it.
- **Rule 3: Keep it polite.** Avoid words like "Failed", "Illegal", or "Prohibited".

**Examples:**
- *Instead of:* "Invalid Date." -> *Use:* "Please select a date in the future."
- *Instead of:* "Network Error 404." -> *Use:* "We're having trouble connecting. Please check your internet and try again."

## 9. Tooltip Text Guidelines

Tooltips should be brief (max 1-2 sentences) and provide helpful context that isn't immediately obvious.
- *Example (Beside 'Shift Preference'):* "Select 'Day' for 12-hour daytime coverage, or '24-hour' for round-the-clock live-in care."
- *Example (Beside 'Mobility Level'):* "Helps us assign a professional trained in the appropriate lifting and moving techniques."

## 13. Deep Dive into Tone by Audience State

The client's emotional state changes during their journey. Our tone must adapt.

### Discovery Phase (Browsing)
- **Client State:** Anxious, seeking reassurance, comparing options.
- **Our Tone:** Informative, confident, calming.
- **Example:** "Learn how our rigorously vetted team provides peace of mind."

### Booking Phase (Filling forms)
- **Client State:** Focused, potentially stressed by details.
- **Our Tone:** Clear, directive, helpful.
- **Example:** "Please list any specific mobility challenges so we can match the right skills."

### Waiting Phase (Under Review)
- **Client State:** Impatient, uncertain.
- **Our Tone:** Transparent, reassuring.
- **Example:** "Our senior nurse is reviewing your case to ensure a perfect fit."

### Active Care Phase (Service ongoing)
- **Client State:** Relief, but monitoring quality.
- **Our Tone:** Supportive, accessible.
- **Example:** "Need to adjust the schedule? Contact your care concierge anytime."

## 14. Handling Difficult Conversations (Support Scripts)

### Late Arrival
"We apologize for the delay. Your professional [Name] is currently [X minutes] away. We are monitoring their progress and will update you shortly."

### Professional Replacement Needed
"To ensure consistent care quality, we need to assign a new professional for your upcoming shift. We have already selected [New Name], who has the exact qualifications required for your care plan."

### Billing Disputes
"I understand your concern regarding this invoice. Let me review the shift logs and care plan immediately. I will get back to you with a detailed breakdown within [Timeframe]."

## 15. Cultural and Localization Considerations for Delhi NCR

While the primary language is English, consider the following regional nuances:

- **Addresses:** Be prepared for complex address structures (e.g., Block, Phase, Sector, Plot). Provide ample space in forms and don't restrict special characters.
- **Titles:** Use standard professional titles (Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.). Avoid overly familiar terms until a relationship is established.
- **Holidays:** Acknowledge major regional festivals if they impact service availability or scheduling, but maintain a professional, non-intrusive tone.

## 16. Extensive Error Message Permutations

### Form Validation
- "First name must contain only letters."
- "Please select a relationship from the dropdown list."
- "The selected date conflicts with an existing booking."
- "Address line 1 is required to dispatch care."
- "Please accept the terms of service to proceed."

### System Errors
- "We are experiencing a temporary network issue. Please try saving again."
- "Your session has expired for security reasons. Please log in again."
- "This file is too large. Please upload an image smaller than 5MB."
- "We couldn't process this payment. Please check with your bank or try another method."
- "The requested page is unavailable. Return to your dashboard."

## 19. Edge Case Messaging
- Account Locked: "Too many failed attempts. Your account is locked for 30 minutes."
- Payment Failed: "The transaction was declined by your bank. Please use another card."
- Service Unavailable: "We currently do not offer this service in your pin code."
- Overlapping Bookings: "You already have an active booking for this time slot."
- Maintenance Mode: "CareConnect is undergoing scheduled maintenance. Please check back in an hour."
