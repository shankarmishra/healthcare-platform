# Personas and Point-of-View Analysis

This document outlines the Point-of-View (POV) analyses for 20 unique perspectives interacting with the Healthcare Staffing & Home Care Platform. Each perspective is detailed to understand their goals, contexts, needs, and potential pain points, ensuring the platform is designed to cater to all users effectively.

## A. Client Booking Care

| Field | Description |
|---|---|

He is tech-savvy, values time and efficiency, and is currently looking to book a trusted physiotherapy service for his mother who is recovering from a minor joint issue. He expects a seamless, transparent, and quick booking process without hidden surprises. |
| **Goal** | To quickly find, vet, and book a qualified, reliable physiotherapist for his mother, ensuring the professional arrives on time and provides high-quality care, all while being able to track the visit progress remotely while he is at work. |
| **Context** | Using the platform primarily during short breaks at his IT job or during his commute. | **Primary Information Needs** | - Clear list of available services and specialized physiotherapy options.<br>- Verified profiles of healthcare professionals, complete with ratings, reviews, and credentials.<br>- Transparent pricing structures, including any taxes or additional fees.<br>- Real-time availability of the professionals and estimated time of arrival.<br>- Clear cancellation and refund policies. |
| **Primary Actions** | - Searching for "Physiotherapy for Seniors" in his local area.<br>- Filtering professionals based on gender preference, ratings, and availability.<br>- Selecting a suitable time slot for the visit.<br>- Completing a secure digital payment or selecting a reliable pay-later option.<br>- Tracking the booking status from requested to completed. |
| **Fear / Risk** | - The professional might not be properly verified, putting his mother's health and safety at risk.<br>- Hidden costs that appear only at the final checkout step.<br>- The professional arriving late or cancelling at the last minute while he is away at work.<br>- The care provided not being up to standard, causing harm or discomfort to his mother. |
| **Expected Reassurance** | - Prominent display of "Verified Professional" badges and clear credential summaries.<br>- Clear, step-by-step upfront pricing before any payment details are entered.<br>- Real-time notifications (SMS/App) confirming the booking, dispatch, and arrival of the professional.<br>- A clear channel to contact support or the professional directly if issues arise. |

| **Required UI Feedback** | - Immediate, visual confirmation of successful payment and booking creation.<br>- Progress tracker for the booking state (e.g., "Assigned", "On the Way", "Checked In").<br>- Clear error states if a search yields no results, along with suggestions to broaden the search criteria. |
| **Next Desired Action** | - Receive a confirmation summary that he can easily share with his mother via WhatsApp.<br>- Set up a recurring schedule if the first session is successful.<br>- Leave a review for the professional post-visit. |
